Not included: Apple's Dictionary Development Kit
------------------------------------------------
StarDict2Mac runs Apple's Dictionary Development Kit for the final build step,
but the kit is not part of StarDict2Mac, because Apple's terms don't allow
redistributing it. The app uses a copy the user installed from Apple's
"Additional Tools for Xcode", or, on request, downloads the kit's files from
https://github.com/nanoskript/dictionary-development-kit (a copy of the kit in
Additional Tools for Xcode 14.1) and checks each file's SHA-256 fingerprint.
The Dictionary Development Kit is © Apple Inc. and subject to Apple's terms.
